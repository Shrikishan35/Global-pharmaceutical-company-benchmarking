Global Pharmaceutical Company Benchmarking
Eli Lilly and Company vs Pfizer Inc. | FY2021–FY2025
Project Overview
This project presents a comparative analysis of Eli Lilly and Company and Pfizer Inc. over FY2021–FY2025, focusing on financial performance, product portfolios, revenue growth and business positioning.

The analysis uses company-reported financial and product revenue data extracted from SEC filings and annual reports. Python and Pandas were used for data extraction, cleaning, transformation and analysis, while Power BI was used to develop interactive dashboards.

Project Objectives
Compare five-year financial performance and revenue growth.
Analyse profitability, R&D expenditure and operating cash flow.
Evaluate product-level revenue trends and portfolio composition.
Assess product revenue concentration and key growth contributors.
Identify strategic differences and business considerations.

Key Findings
Eli Lilly
Revenue increased from approximately $28.32 billion in FY2021 to $65.18 billion in FY2025.
Five-year revenue CAGR was approximately 23.17%.
Mounjaro and Zepbound together contributed approximately 56% of FY2025 total revenue.
Cardiometabolic products became a major contributor to the company's revenue mix.
Pfizer
Revenue decreased from approximately $81.29 billion in FY2021 to $62.58 billion in FY2025.
Five-year revenue CAGR was approximately -6.33%.
The decline reflects the reduction in COVID-related product sales after the pandemic peak.
Oncology and Specialty Care increased their shares of the commercial portfolio between FY2021 and FY2025.
Methodology
Extracted financial and product revenue information from SEC company filings.
Cleaned and transformed the extracted data using Python and Pandas.
Validated extracted revenue totals against reported company figures.
Calculated financial indicators, revenue growth and product concentration.
Imported the prepared datasets into Power BI.
Developed comparative dashboards and interpreted the findings.
Summarised business implications and recommendations in a presentation.

Important Considerations & Limitations
Product-level disclosures and classifications may change across reporting years.
Missing product disclosures are not automatically treated as zero.
Therapeutic-area classifications for Eli Lilly and commercial portfolio classifications for Pfizer are different and are not directly equivalent.
Pfizer R&D expenditure excludes acquired in-process research and development (IPR&D) in this analysis.
Small differences between extracted and reported revenue totals may arise from rounding.
Historical financial performance does not guarantee future performance.
